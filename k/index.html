KURORSAHI MULTI-SPORT ACADEMY - STUDENT GUIDELINES

1. Code of Conduct and Discipline:

Respect instructors, fellow students, and all academy staff at all times.

Maintain a positive attitude and be open to learning and improvement.

Avoid disruptive behavior that could impact the learning environment.

Show sportsmanship, regardless of the outcome in any practice or competition.

2. Attendance and Punctuality:

Arrive at least 10 minutes before class to warm up and be prepared.

Attend all scheduled classes consistently to ensure steady progress.

Inform the instructor in advance if unable to attend a class.

Maintain regular attendance to qualify for belt tests, promotions, and competitions.

3. Training Expectations and Progress Tracking:

Follow the training plan provided by the instructor diligently.

Stay committed to the practice routine, both in class and at home.

Participate actively in classes and maintain focus during training sessions.

Progress is monitored regularly; students will receive constructive feedback to improve.

4. Dress Code and Equipment:

Wear the designated uniform and protective gear as instructed.

Keep uniforms clean, well-maintained, and free of offensive prints or language.

Bring personal water bottles and necessary training equipment to every class.

Ensure that personal belongings are properly labeled and stored safely.

5. Hygiene and Cleanliness:

Maintain personal hygiene to prevent the spread of infections.

Keep fingernails and toenails trimmed to avoid injuries.

Wash hands before and after training sessions.

Report any skin infections, injuries, or medical conditions to the instructor.

6. Safety Protocols and Injury Management:

Inform the instructor immediately if feeling unwell or injured.

Avoid attempting techniques beyond your current skill level without supervision.

Follow the instructor's guidance during sparring or combat drills to prevent injuries.

Use protective gear as instructed and ensure it is in good condition.

7. Respect for Instructors and Fellow Students:

Follow the instructor's guidance and show appreciation for their efforts.

Encourage fellow students and practice teamwork during group activities.

Avoid inappropriate language or gestures that may offend others.

Resolve conflicts peacefully and seek guidance from instructors if needed.

8. Communication and Feedback:

Approach instructors respectfully with questions, concerns, or feedback.

Participate in feedback sessions and assessments to track progress.

Keep parents/guardians informed about training schedules and progress (for child students).

For any queries or concerns, students can contact the academy office or their respective instructor.
