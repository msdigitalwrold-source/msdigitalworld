Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our taxi service in Puri

What is the taxi fare from Bhubaneswar Airport to Puri?

The taxi fare from Bhubaneswar Airport to Puri ranges from ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 depending on the car type. AC sedan costs around ₹1,800, SUV like Innova costs around ₹2,500. The journey takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours covering 60 km distance.

What are the best tour packages in Puri?

The most popular tour packages include: 1) Jagannath Temple Darshan Package, 2) Golden Triangle Odisha Tour (Puri-Konark-Bhubaneswar), 3) Chilika Lake Day Trip, 4) Konark Sun Temple Tour, and 5) Complete Spiritual Odisha Tour covering all major temples.

Do you provide tempo traveller for group tours?

Yes, we provide 12-seater and 17-seater tempo travellers for group tours in Odisha. Our tempo travellers are well-maintained with AC, push-back seats, and experienced drivers. Ideal for family trips, corporate outings, and pilgrimage tours.

What is the best time to visit Puri?

The best time to visit Puri is from October to March when the weather is pleasant. June-July is ideal for Rath Yatra festival. Avoid monsoon season (August-September) due to heavy rains. Winter months (November-February) are perfect for beach activities and temple visits.

How can I book a taxi in Puri?

Booking is super easy! Just send us a WhatsApp message or call us directly. Share your travel dates, number of passengers, and destinations — we'll provide you the best quote instantly. No advance payment needed for most bookings.

Do you offer outstation taxi services from Puri?

Yes! We offer outstation taxi services from Puri to Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Konark, Chilika, Berhampur, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam and many more destinations. Both one-way and round-trip options available with competitive pricing.
